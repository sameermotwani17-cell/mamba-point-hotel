Our Story

Liberia's Most
Distinguished Hotel

Founded on 21st December 1993 by Anna and Chawki Bsaibes — an Irish-Lebanese couple with a vision for timeless hospitality — Mamba Point Hotel holds the distinction of being the longest established hotel in Liberia.

Set within its own lush grounds overlooking the Atlantic Ocean and located in Monrovia's prestigious Diplomatic Area, we stand just one minute's drive from the city centre. Following sweeping renovations, the hotel now rivals the finest European boutique properties — while retaining its unmistakably warm West African soul.
